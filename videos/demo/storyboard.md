# Native capture storyboard

One continuous native Zen view. Left: 292 px sidebar with glass tabs. Top: compact-mode frame with matching events. Main content: local demo pages with a short chapter title.
Events use the source animation's start/end times and smooth continuous sampling; no generated replacement for browser chrome. During the middle of each event, a detail panel at (805,425), size 710×405, magnifies a 292×150 crop of the real sidebar by approximately 2.3×. The crop follows the source vertically, fades in at 23–32% of the scene and fades out at 68–77%. The full window remains visible. All later tab-switching and usage scenes retain the unmodified full-window composition. Three sidebar tab clicks are captured through Marionette. Download samples are synthetic local fixtures.
